DUSTLINE — a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Two teams fight over a desert outpost at dusk: plant the spike at site A or B, or wipe the other team. First to 5 rounds takes the match. Bots follow routes, hold angles, react to gunfire, plant and defuse; money earned each round buys rifles, SMGs, armour and grenades in the buy menu.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Click to play: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, 1/2 to switch weapons, G to throw a grenade, B for the buy menu, E to plant the spike (hold it inside a site), Shift to sprint, Ctrl to crouch, Space to jump.
